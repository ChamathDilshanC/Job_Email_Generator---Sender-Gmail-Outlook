# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want JobMail collaboration to be welcoming, respectful, and practical. Participants are expected to:

- communicate professionally and assume good intent;
- focus feedback on the code, documentation, or proposal rather than the person;
- explain trade-offs and provide evidence when disagreeing;
- respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or personal data;
- accept maintainers' decisions after concerns have been discussed.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, and disruptive spam.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **dilshancolonne123@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed by the project maintainer and handled proportionately, including warnings, removal of content, or restriction of participation when necessary.
